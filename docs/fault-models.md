# QubitGuard: Fault Taxonomy & Mutation Model

## 1. Mathematical Formalism of Quantum Mutations
In quantum computing, an $n$-qubit quantum program is represented as an ordered sequence of unitary gate applications $\mathcal{U} = \{U_1, U_2, \dots, U_m\}$ acting on state $|\psi_0\rangle = |0\rangle^{\otimes n}$, terminated by computational basis measurement operators $\mathcal{M} = \{M_k\}$.

A software mutation $\mu: \mathcal{U} \to \mathcal{U}'$ represents a single syntactic or semantic deviation in the circuit's description.

---

## 2. Standardized Mutation Operators

| Operator Symbol | Name | Formal Transformation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| $O_{gom}$ | **Gate Omission** | $\mathcal{U}' = \mathcal{U} \setminus \{U_k\}$ | Simulates missing gate logic, forgotten initialization, or dropped entangler. |
| $O_{wg}$ | **Wrong Gate** | $U_k \to V_k, \quad \text{dim}(V_k) = \text{dim}(U_k)$ | Replaces an operation with an incorrect unitary of identical arity ($H \to X$, $CX \to CZ$). |
| $O_{wq}$ | **Wrong Qubit** | $U(q_i) \to U(q_j), \quad i \neq j$ | Directs gate execution to an unintended physical qubit register. |
| $O_{pp}$ | **Parameter Perturbation** | $R_x(\theta) \to R_x(\theta + \delta), \quad \delta \in \mathbb{R}$ | Continuous rotation angle drift in variational or phase estimation ansatzes. |
| $O_{gos}$ | **Gate Order Swap** | $(U_k, U_{k+1}) \to (U_{k+1}, U_k), [U_k, U_{k+1}] \neq 0$ | Reverses execution order of non-commuting operations. |
| $O_{mm}$ | **Measurement Mutation** | $M(q_i, c_i) \to M(q_j, c_i)$ | Taps intermediate or final measurement on the wrong qubit line. |
| $O_{co}$ | **Controlled Operation** | $U(q_i) \to C_U(q_c, q_i)$ | Accidentally introduces control condition from an ancillary qubit. |
| $O_{rg}$ | **Redundant Gate** | $\mathcal{U}' = \mathcal{U} \cup \{V\}, \quad V \neq I$ | Injects superfluous Pauli or Hadamard gate causing unexpected phase or bit error. |
