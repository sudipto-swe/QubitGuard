# QubitGuard: Automated Quantum Program Repair (Q-APR)

## 1. Formulation of the Quantum Repair Problem
Given:
1. A faulty quantum program $P_{faulty}$ with detected semantic discrepancies.
2. A quantum test suite $\mathcal{T} = \{t_1, t_2, \dots, t_k\}$ (metamorphic and property tests).
3. A fault localization ranking specifying suspicious gate indices $\{g_{(1)}, g_{(2)}, \dots, g_{(m)}\}$.

The objective of Quantum Automated Program Repair (Q-APR) is to find a candidate modification $\Delta$ such that:
$$P_{repaired} = P_{faulty} \oplus \Delta, \quad \forall t \in \mathcal{T}, \quad t(P_{repaired}) = \text{PASS}$$
and $P_{repaired}$ restores state fidelity against reference invariants.

---

## 2. Search Space & Mutation Primitives
Candidate patches are generated by perturbing operations localized to the top suspicious gates:
- **Gate Deletion**: Prunes an extraneous or redundant gate ($O_{del}$).
- **Gate Replacement**: Substitutes an erroneous gate with an admissible gate of equal arity from the search bank $\{H, X, Y, Z, S, T\}$ or $\{CX, CZ, \text{SWAP}\}$.
- **Qubit Target Swap**: Reverses control and target qubit registers on two-qubit gates.
- **Parameter Re-tuning**: Discretized angle optimization over continuous rotation gates ($\delta \in \{\pm 0.1\pi, \pm 0.25\pi, \pm 0.5\pi\}$).

---

## 3. Dual-Tier Validation & Overfitting Mitigation
A central peril in classical and quantum APR is **patch overfitting**, where a synthesized candidate satisfies the test suite through coincidental cancellation without achieving true semantic correctness.

QubitGuard enforces two-tiered validation:
1. **Tier 1 (Test-Adequacy)**:
   Candidate patch passes 100% of metamorphic and invariant assertions in test suite $\mathcal{T}$.
2. **Tier 2 (Distribution & State Fidelity Validation)**:
   Candidate patch must demonstrate Total Variation Distance $\text{TVD}(P_{repaired}, P_{ref}) \le 0.08$ on independent holdout evaluations.

Only candidates satisfying both criteria are certified as **Validated Repairs**.
